# Being Just Dangerous Enough

You may think that in the age of AI, knowing everything is the ultimate flex. Let me tell you this clearly and loudly: no, it isn't. That is the old way of thinking. Knowing everything to a tee. Sharpening your blade daily to keep your skills razor sharp. Being the guy who knows it all.

Sorry mate, you missed the memo.

Now everyone is a rockstar. A deadly ninja. A one man army. An infinite wealth of trivia. But there is a catch. All of these amazing capabilities are hosted offshore, somewhere in the cloud, and your superpowers are on a monthly subscription. As long as you keep paying the LLM overlords, you get to stay a superhero. You are one unrecoverable disaster away from turning back into a primitive primate. Yes, you heard that right. But what are the chances? Very, very slim, I would say. In engineering terms, we are talking about the same five nines of availability we used to chase in our SLOs. The cloud gods rarely blink.

So here is my point, and I will keep it short. You don't need to know all the nooks and crannies any more. Be able to adapt. Be able to learn. Don't go too deep, but make sure there is width. Depth is now a prompt away.

Be just dangerous enough. The rest is your LLM buddy, your loyal agent, there to augment you.

And maybe keep one good book on the shelf. You know, for the day the cloud blinks.
